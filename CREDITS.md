# Drawing references

The landmark outlines in `traces.js` were extracted from these public references (edge or mask tracing, then Ramer–Douglas–Peucker simplification) and drawn in the site's line style. They are not copies of trademarked logos.

- Boeing 737-800 side elevation: traced from the linework in [Boeing 737-800 v1.0.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boeing_737-800_v1.0.png) by Julien Scavini, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- LSU Memorial Tower: silhouette traced from [LSU Memorial Tower.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LSU_Memorial_Tower.jpg), public domain.
- Tiger Stadium: silhouette traced from [LSU Tiger Stadium.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LSU_Tiger_Stadium.jpg) by Ken Lund, CC BY-SA 2.0.
- Mercedes-Benz Superdome roof: silhouette traced from [New Orleans Central Business District 28 Oct 2024 - Superdome.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:New_Orleans_Central_Business_District_28_Oct_2024_-_Superdome.jpg), CC BY-SA 4.0.
- St. Charles streetcar: silhouette traced from [Streetcar 962, St Charles Avenue, New Orleans, 1990s.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Streetcar_962,_St_Charles_Avenue,_New_Orleans,_1990s.jpg) by Infrogmation, CC BY 2.0.
- New Orleans house: silhouette of a raised Irish Channel cottage (porch, columns, gable), not Colin's home, traced from [Shotgun House, Irish Channel, New Orleans 01.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shotgun_House,_Irish_Channel,_New_Orleans_01.jpg) by Infrogmation, CC BY 2.0.
- Starlink Standard user terminal: the plain rectangular face and kickstand, from SpaceX's published product photographs. No antenna grid is drawn.
- Certification emblems are original geometric sketches (a cloud, a ring, a star, a node diagram, a shield, a check). They are not the AWS, Scaled Agile, Google, CompTIA, ISC2, or ISACA marks.
