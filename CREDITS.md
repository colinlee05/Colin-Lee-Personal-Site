# Drawing references

The landmark sketches are original line drawings made for this site. They are not copies of trademarked logos or official badge art. Proportions follow public descriptions and photographs:

- Boeing 737-800 side profile: fuselage fineness, nose ogive, wing position, and the CFM56 nacelle's flattened lower lip, from publicly described aircraft geometry and side-view photographs on Wikimedia Commons (for example, photographs of N73700-series aircraft under Creative Commons licenses).
- Mercedes-Benz Superdome: the shallow roof rise, concentric rings, and facade band, from exterior photographs on Wikimedia Commons such as views of the Louisiana Superdome.
- Starlink Standard user terminal: the plain rectangular face and kickstand, from SpaceX's published product photographs. No antenna grid is drawn.
- Perley Thomas streetcars on the St. Charles line: the arched window bays, clerestory, single trolley pole, and trucks, from historic and contemporary photographs of the New Orleans streetcars.
- Tiger Stadium and the LSU Memorial Tower: the bowl, light towers, campanile shaft, clock, and pyramidal roof, from photographs of the Baton Rouge campus.
- Certification emblems are original geometric sketches (a cloud, a ring, a star, a node diagram, a shield, a check). They are not the AWS, Scaled Agile, Google, CompTIA, ISC2, or ISACA marks.
